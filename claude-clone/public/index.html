About Alcove

A clean-room rebuild of the signed-in assistant workspace, written from public documentation and screenshots. It rebuilds the features and the flow, not the original's code, brand, copy or content.

Version1.0
RepliesScripted engine, streamed
StorageCloudflare D1, per account
Original's modelNot included

No analytics, no third-party requests, no advertising. The only cookies are the session cookie.
