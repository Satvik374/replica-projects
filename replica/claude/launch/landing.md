# Landing page — Alcove

Built from the recommended angle in `../fixes.md`. Copy is in the brand voice:
plain, unhurried, specific. No invented testimonials, no user counts, no star
ratings, no press logos, and no mention of the original anywhere.

## 1. Hero

- **Headline:** A thinking space that shows the budget before you spend it.
- **Sub:** Chats, projects and artifacts in one calm workspace. One message, one
  count, and a reset time you can plan around. No peak-hour tax.
- **Primary button:** Start a workspace
- **Secondary:** See how the count works
- **Visual:** a real screenshot of a chat with the sidebar usage meter visible —
  the number is the product, so it is in the picture.

## 2. The problem

Two themes from the research, paraphrased rather than quoted:

- **The wall arrives without warning.** You are mid-project, the reply stops, and
  the only useful information is a reset time you have to go looking for. The same
  hour of work can cost different amounts depending on the time of day.
- **Nobody wants unlimited, they want predictable.** People are doing the
  accounting by hand — trimming files, editing instead of following up, starting
  new threads to save context. That is the product's job, not the user's.

## 3. How it works

1. **Ask.** One message, one count against your window. The number is on screen.
2. **Keep it.** Chats, projects with their own knowledge, and artifacts that keep
   their versions.
3. **Share or leave.** A public link is a snapshot you can revoke, and export is
   one click. Deleting your account deletes your data.

## 6. FAQ

- **Is there a model behind this?** No. This is a clean-room study of a chat
  workspace; replies come from a scripted engine so the interface and the limits
  can be judged honestly.
- **What counts as a message?** One message you send. Attachments and replies do
  not count separately.
- **When does the window reset?** Five hours after the window started. The exact
  time is in Settings → Usage.
- **Can I get my data out?** Yes — one JSON file with everything, from Privacy.
- **Can I import from another assistant?** Not yet. The export shape is public and
  an importer is the next thing on the list.
- **Does it train on my chats?** There is no training pipeline here, and no
  third-party request leaves the app.

## 7. Final call to action

**Start a workspace.** One line under the button: "Free to try. Export anything,
delete everything, cancel in one click."
